# Project Case Studies

## Egytronic_1.0

An 8B-parameter decoder-only language model fine-tuned for Egyptian Arabic and Egyptian/local linguistic contexts. The work covered multilingual instruction-data preparation, Alpaca-style formatting, fine-tuning with Hugging Face Transformers, Unsloth, and PyTorch, plus F16 and GGUF publication for practical inference workflows.

## Azura Cafe & Restaurant

A mobile-first restaurant experience accessed through QR codes. The product turns a conventional menu into short-form, reel-style browsing, with Firebase Realtime Database synchronization, administrative workflows, AI assistance powered primarily by Groq and Llama 3.3, and Cloudflare Pages/R2 deployment.

## SmartBoard AI

An AI-powered educational workspace for teachers and students. It supports lesson generation, reusable educational content, asset generation, LaTeX, text-to-speech, 3D educational examples, and interactive teaching modes.
